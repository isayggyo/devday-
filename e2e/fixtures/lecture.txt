This lecture explains retrieval practice and spaced repetition.
Retrieval practice strengthens memory by recalling information without looking at notes.
Active recall is different from rereading. Close the book, answer a question, then check the answer.
Spaced repetition distributes practice across several days.
Review material after one day, three days, and seven days.
The schedule is an example, not a universal rule.
Working memory has limited capacity.
A visual slide should focus on one central idea and show its source page.
Short explanations help learners connect spoken examples with the lecture material.
Combine an active recall question with a spaced review plan.
